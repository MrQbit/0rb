Generate a key at login.tailscale.com → Generate auth key. The orb joins your tailnet and serves itself over HTTPS.

Public access

Off = only devices on your tailnet can reach the URL. On = it works from anywhere on the internet (Tailscale Funnel) — sign-in is still required, but the door is public. The phone apps need this ON to work away from home without the Tailscale app.

Device URL—

This box's registered name with a real certificate.

Where it points

Home network = zero-warning HTTPS at home; use Tailscale when away. Internet = the address follows your public IP (refreshed every 10 minutes) and your router forwards port 9444 — the orb opens it itself when the router allows (UPnP), or shows exact steps for yours.

Self-signed — accept the cert once for camera/mic on the LAN.
